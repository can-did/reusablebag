01 · Problem

Reusable bags existed, but remembering them was the real challenge.

The everyday grocery pattern was simple: customers intended to be more sustainable, yet the default checkout behavior often ended with buying another disposable plastic bag. The project needed to make the reusable bag memorable, useful, and economically rewarding.

Behavioral insight A shopper at home does not reduce plastic. A shopper remembered at checkout does.

02 · Solution

A bag with a built-in reason to bring it back.

The Bonus Bag connected a physical reusable shopper to VkusVill’s loyalty system through a QR-code interaction. Customers scanned the bag at checkout and received bonuses instead of buying a plastic bag, transforming a sustainability choice into a repeatable loyalty moment.

  • Simple physical cue: a branded reusable bag with an obvious QR code.
  • Immediate reward: bonuses credited for choosing the reusable option.
  • Clear value story: savings on plastic bags plus loyalty points.

03 · How it worked

From checkout action to habit loop.

1

Bring the reusable bag

The customer carries the Bonus Bag instead of taking a new disposable plastic bag.

2

Scan the bag QR code

At checkout, the bag’s QR code is scanned alongside the customer loyalty card.

3

Receive bonuses

The customer receives 6 bonuses for not using a plastic bag, making the sustainable action measurable.

4

Repeat the behavior

The reward reinforces the habit and gives customers a reason to keep the bag in daily circulation.

04 · User incentives

The project made sustainability feel practical rather than abstract.

Customers were motivated by a layered value proposition: they avoided paying for a disposable bag, collected loyalty bonuses, and could mentally translate repeated use into everyday grocery items. The mechanic reduced friction by fitting into the existing checkout and loyalty-card flow.

Money saved Bonuses earned Visible eco-action Habit formation

05 · Impact & business value

A small checkout interaction created value for customers, brand, and operations.

Lower plastic-bag dependency

Every rewarded scan encouraged customers to replace a single-use bag with a reusable alternative.

Stronger loyalty engagement

The mechanic gave the loyalty card another meaningful use case beyond discounts and points accumulation.

Memorable brand behavior

The bag became a visible reminder of VkusVill’s eco-positioning and customer-centric reward design.

Measurable campaign loop

QR scans created a trackable touchpoint that could connect sustainable behavior to purchase frequency.
